Neon Bastion Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Overview

Neon Bastion is an offline, single-player game. This policy describes its actual local data practices.

Information accessed, collected and transmitted

The game does not collect or transmit personal or sensitive user information. It does not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, telephone information or advertising identifiers. No account is required. The app has no Internet permission.

Local game data

Game progress and preferences are stored in the application's private storage on your device to support saving and continuing play. This may include the current game state, unlocked achievements or medals, and sound preferences. This information stays on your device; the developer does not receive it. App backup and device transfer of app data are disabled by the app's Android configuration.

Advertising, analytics, purchases and third parties

The game includes no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, third-party tracking, account system, in-app purchases or remote server. It does not share game data with third parties. Google Play and the Android operating system may independently process information under their own privacy policies; such processing is not controlled by this game.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you delete it. Use the game's Settings → Delete all local progress to remove game progress. Sound settings can remain after this action. To remove all app data and preferences, use Android Settings → Apps → Neon Bastion → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. No server-side account or data deletion request is needed because no game account or server storage exists.

Security

The app uses Android application-private storage, requests no sensitive runtime permissions, and does not transmit game data. Device access and security remain subject to your device's settings and protections.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from any user, including children.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated if the game's data practices change. The revised effective date will be shown above. New SDKs, online features or advertisements require a fresh review of this policy and the Play Data safety declaration before release.
